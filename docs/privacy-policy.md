# Privacy policy

Ride or Nah tells you whether today is a good day to ride, using limits you set. It's built to work entirely on your iPhone. We don't run servers, we don't have accounts, and we don't collect anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no personal data, usage data, diagnostics or identifiers from the app. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your iPhone

- Your profiles, limits, ride windows, saved places and notification settings.
- Your approximate location, used to find the forecast where you are. Where you last opened the app is kept on your iPhone only, so the morning notification can use it.
- Cached forecasts.

## What goes to Apple, and why

The app only talks to Apple services:

- **Apple Weather (WeatherKit):** to get forecasts, the app sends Apple the coordinates of the points it checks (your place and the points around your ride radius, rounded to about 1 km). Apple's own privacy policy covers this.
- **iCloud:** if you're signed in to iCloud, your settings sync between your own devices through your iCloud account. We can't see them.
- **Apple Maps:** when you search for a place to save, the search text goes to Apple Maps.
- **App Store:** purchases, trials, restores and subscription management are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account.

Calendar suggestions are added through the system's own add-event screen. The app never reads your calendar.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
